        Sync notes with GitHub

        Your highlights, notes, reading progress and list of saved papers go to a private gist, texglass-notes.json, in your own GitHub account, and come back on every device where you sign in. Nothing passes through any other server. Notes made on two devices at once are merged. You sign in once: after that everything syncs automatically.
