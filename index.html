      Sync notes with GitHub

      Your highlights and notes go to a private gist, texglass-notes.json, in your own GitHub account, and come back on every device where you sign in. Nothing passes through any other server. Notes made on two devices at once are merged.

      Open from this computer

      Choose a .tex file, or the folder it is in to bring in its \input files, bibliography and figures. You can also drop either on this page.

      To view .tex files that you open in the browser itself (file:// addresses), turn on Allow access to file URLs for TeXglass in your extension settings: chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions), then Details.
