TeXglass for arXiv

Open papers in

Click the toolbar button on an arXiv page, right-click a link to an arXiv paper, or use the View in TeXglass links on arxiv.org, and on alphaXiv, HAL and ResearchGate pages of papers that are also on arXiv. TeXglass downloads the LaTeX source from arxiv.org/src, picks the main file and inlines its \input and \include files.

Keyboard shortcut: Alt+Shift+K does what the toolbar button does: it opens the paper of the page you are on, or else TeXglass with its arXiv search. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts (Edge: edge://extensions/shortcuts; Firefox: Manage Extension Shortcuts in the gear menu of about:addons). Inside TeXglass, Ctrl+K opens the arXiv search.
